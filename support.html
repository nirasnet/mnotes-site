Frequently asked

The live transcript is empty or stops. Thai and several other languages are recognised by Apple's servers, so recording needs an internet connection. Audio is always saved; open the recording and tap Re-transcribe Audio once you are online.

Notes don't appear on my other device. Both devices must be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on. Pull down on the home screen to sync now, and check Settings → iCloud for the last upload, download and any error.

How do I write with my finger? Switch the note to Edit mode with the pencil toggle in the toolbar. View mode scrolls and flips pages with your fingers.

How do I hear what was said when I wrote something? Open a recording and choose Play with notes; tap any line of ink to jump there.

How do I export? Tap Share in a note for PDF, Markdown, transcript, summary or audio.

Requirements

iPadOS 18 or later, iOS 18 or later. Apple Pencil is recommended on iPad. Summaries with Apple Intelligence need iOS 26 on a supported device; other devices use an offline summary.

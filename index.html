Our story

mnotes started in a lecture hall, with a student trying to do three things at once: write down what the professor said, photograph the slide before it changed, and remember the one sentence that explained everything. No app did all three without turning into a toolbox.

So mnotes keeps it minimal. You open a page and write. Tap once to drop the slide onto the page, tap once to record. While you write, the lecture is transcribed, and when it ends you get a short summary in plain words. Your ink is tied to the audio, so later you can tap a line you wrote and hear what was being said at that moment.

Nothing is uploaded to anyone but you: notes live on your device and in your own iCloud, so your iPad and iPhone always show the same notebooks. No accounts, no ads, no feed.

The idea in five lines

Write first. The page is the whole screen. Tools appear only when you pick up the Pencil.

Capture in one tap. Slides, whiteboards and handouts land on the page next to your handwriting.

Listen later. Your ink is synced to the recording — tap any line to replay that moment.

Understand faster. A transcript while the lecture runs, a summary when it ends, in Thai or English.

Yours only. On-device recognition and your private iCloud. Nobody else sees a thing.

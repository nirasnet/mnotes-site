Privacy Policy

mnotes · Minimal notes

Last updated 15 October 2026

mnotes is built so that your notes stay yours. This policy explains what the app does with your data, in plain words.

What mnotes stores

Notebooks, handwritten pages, photos you add, audio recordings, transcripts and summaries are stored on your device. If you keep Sync with iCloud turned on, the same data is also stored in the private iCloud database that belongs to your Apple Account, so your iPad and iPhone show the same notes. We, the developer, cannot read that database.

What we collect

Nothing. mnotes has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run servers that receive your notes, recordings or usage data.

Speech recognition and summaries

Live transcription uses Apple's Speech framework. For some languages (for example Thai) recognition is performed by Apple's servers; audio for those requests is sent to Apple under Apple's privacy policy and is not sent to us. Summaries are generated on your device with Apple Intelligence where available, or with an offline method otherwise.

Permissions

Microphone and speech recognition are used only while you record. Camera and photo library access are used only when you add a photo or scan a document. You can revoke any of these in the Settings app.

Sharing

When you export a note, transcript, summary or recording, it is handed to the app or person you choose through the iOS share sheet. mnotes never shares anything on its own.

Deleting your data

Deleting a note or notebook removes it from the device and, when sync is on, from your iCloud database. Deleting the app removes all local data. You can also turn off iCloud sync in Settings → iCloud.

Children

mnotes does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal data at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
